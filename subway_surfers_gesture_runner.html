Gesture Controls Guide

Move Left / Right

Shift your hand horizontally to the left 35% or right 35% of the camera frame to switch lanes.

Jump

Raise your hand up high into the top 30% of the screen or point your index finger upward.

Duck / Slide

Lower your hand into the bottom 30% of the frame or lower your wrist quickly.

Alternative Keyboard Controls

You can also use A / D or Left / Right Arrows to switch lanes, W / Up Arrow to Jump, and S / Down Arrow to Duck.
